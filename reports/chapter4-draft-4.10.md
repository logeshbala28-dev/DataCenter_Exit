# 4.10 Earned value

**Method.** I measured earned value in hours, because the WBS baseline is in hours. Earned value (EV) is the expected hours of each work package times the share I completed. Actual cost (AC) is my reconstructed attended hours (section 4.9). The unattended initial synchronization is excluded from both sides. Where a deliverable was produced late and in a reduced form (CR-001), I counted it at 50%.

| WBS element | Planned (h) | % complete | EV (h) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 Program planning | 37.6 | 78% (the lean business case at 50%; stakeholder register missing) | 29.5 |
| 2.0 Discovery and wave planning | 33.5 | 25% (pathfinder selection; the lean inventory and wave plan at 50%) | 8.4 |
| 3.0 Landing zone | 50.3 | 100% | 50.3 |
| 4.0 Pathfinder (attended) | 54.5 | 100% | 54.5 |
| 5.0 Resilience and recovery | 26.2 | 84% (controlled failure tests not done) | 21.9 |
| 6.0 Measurement and re-estimation | 28.8 | 0% | 0.0 |
| 7.0 Project control | 32.1 | 70% | 22.5 |
| 8.0 Reporting and closure | 51.5 | 25% | 12.9 |
| **Total** | **314.5** | **64%** | **200.0** |

**Indicators at 16 Nov.**

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Budget at completion (BAC) | 314.5 h |
| Earned value (EV) | 200.0 h |
| Actual cost (AC), including about 30 h of AWS learning | about 170 h |
| Planned value (PV), assumptions below | 285.9 h |
| Cost performance index (CPI = EV / AC) | 1.18 |
| Schedule performance index (SPI = EV / PV) | 0.70 |
| Estimate at completion (EAC = AC + BAC - EV) | about 284.5 h |
| Variance at completion (BAC - EAC) | about +30 h |

PV assumes elements 1.0 to 6.0 were fully planned by now, 7.0 about 10/11 planned, and 8.0 about half planned. Excluding the 30 h of AWS learning, which is not in the WBS, the CPI is 1.43.

**Reading the result.** I read the CPI of 1.18 as cautious estimates and recalled hours, not as efficiency. The SPI of 0.70 is the real signal: four milestones slipped by 2 to 3 weeks (M-3 by 2, M-4 and M-5 by 3, M-6 by 2) and the two buffer weeks are used up. About 114 h of planned work remains (6.0, part of 1.0, 2.0, 7.0 and 8.0), and I cannot deliver all of it in the 7 days left. The remaining scope is therefore reduced through change control, as the lean plan in CR-001 already does for the planning documents.

**Limitations.** The actual hours are mostly recalled. The percentages for 2.0, 7.0 and 8.0 are my own judgments, and the planned-value profile was not recorded in the baseline, so the SPI depends on my assumption.
