# 4.9 Effort

**Method.** No effort log was kept during the project, so I reconstructed my hours afterwards. Where the record has timestamps (commits, screenshots, console logs), I used the span between the first and last logged event as my attended time. Where it does not, I used my own recollection, and I label each such figure as recalled. The count is my own attended time only. Time that ran unattended, such as the initial synchronization of about 111 minutes, is excluded.

**Results.**

| Item | Estimate (pre-registered) | Actual | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wave 0 landing zone (E-9, WBS 3.0) | 50.3 h | about 32.3 h | 64% |
| Wave 1 pathfinder (E-10, WBS 4.0, attended) | 54.5 h | about 29.7 h | 54% |

Wave 0 is 3.3 h on 29 Oct, about 16 h of Terraform code in October and about 13 h of Terraform practice. The pathfinder is 5.5 h (4 Nov), 6.8 h (6 Nov), 4.5 h (11 Nov), 1.9 h (16 Nov morning) and about 11 h of runbook work. All recorded and recalled hours together come to about 170 h, against a baseline of about 314 attended hours.

**Reading the result.** Both estimates were higher than the hours I recorded. I read this as the estimates being cautious, not as the work being finished early. The baseline assumed about 35 hours a week, and my recalled effort was lower in most weeks.

**Limitations.** Most figures are recalled, not measured. The 4 Nov figure is an estimate. About 40 h in October and about 62 h in the days with no commits are recollections, and some of them may overlap with other rows. The October split between documents and code is an estimate I adopted. The disaster recovery work (WBS 5.0) is not included in either comparison.
